It reads the file as it is

Comma, semicolon, tab or pipe; quoted fields; a header buried under a vendor's report title; nested JSON; NULL and N/A markers. Detected, not configured.

🧭

It works out what columns mean

Which column is the name, which is the place, which is the state — inferred from the column names and the values, then shown to you so you can correct it.

🏢

Your layout, not a template

Hangar and apron, or aisles and loading dock, or blocks and a crane pad. The areas are your site; the file's locations get routed into them.

👁️

People, not bar charts

Rows become things anyone recognizes — a person at a desk, a van on the road, an aircraft on the apron — with colour showing state at a glance.

In this build

The demo above is a real production system, proof that the pattern works end-to-end.

  • 🕑 Attendance punches place every employee at their desk by role.
  • 🎓 A training/LMS system's flight hours drive which lessons are running.
  • 📡 Live aircraft radar (ADS-B) confirms which are actually airborne and flies them around the pattern.
  • 🚗 GPS-tracked vehicles appear in the lot via geofence, and drive in when they arrive.
  • ⏪ A day-replay reconstructs the whole day from historical data.

What's under the hood

🔌

Any source, one adapter

Each system sits behind one clean method — including a GPS fleet API with short-lived tokens minted server-side, so no secret ever reaches the browser.

⚡

Real-time

Each feed polls on its own cadence and re-renders continuously — the operation as it is right now, not a nightly report.

🧭

Correlation & flags

Cross-referencing sources surfaces what no single system shows — a resource double-booked, or an asset that just arrived via geofence.

🔐

Role & location security

A permissions model scopes every view by role and by site, so each person sees exactly what they're cleared for.

⏪

Replay

A scrubber reconstructs the whole day from history — rewind to any moment.

📱

Runs anywhere

Pure web — no app to deploy, no plugins. Wall display, laptop, or phone.
